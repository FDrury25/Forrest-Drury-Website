About

Background

Third-year Physics B.S. student at UC Santa Barbara and Aerodynamics Team Lead for Gaucho Racing FSAE.

My work covers the full aerodynamic workflow: CAD in SOLIDWORKS, unstructured meshing, steady and transient RANS in Ansys Fluent on HPC clusters, composite manufacturing, and testing on the car.

I have also completed two undergraduate research projects on FSAE underbody and diffuser aerodynamics, and I drive the car in competition.
